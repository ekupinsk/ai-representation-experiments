LIMITATIONS
  - One dataset (AG News, English, 4 topics), mean pooling only, k-NN overlap as the main metric.
  - Agreement in neighbour structure does not by itself show shared meaning or shared features.
  - 2 random-initialisation seed(s) per architecture; one data sample.
  - Hidden states were standardized per dimension; raw results are reported in Experiment 4.
